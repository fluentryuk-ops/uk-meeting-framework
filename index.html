01 Polite Disagreement

Without sounding rude — four registers, one intention.

Friendly · Safe disagreement "I see your point, but…"
Professional · Neutral tone "I'm not sure I completely agree."
Formal · Controlled disagreement "With respect, I disagree."
Formal · Executive tone "I'm not entirely convinced that approach would achieve the desired outcome."
02 Softening Opinions

Confidence without sounding weak.

Friendly · Lowers pressure "I might be wrong, however…"
Professional · Thoughtful input "From my perspective, this may be worth considering."
Formal · Serious warning "It would appear that this option carries certain risks."
Formal · Board-level phrasing "This proposal may benefit from further evaluation before a final decision is made."
03 Asking for Clarification

Reduce anxiety in meetings.

Friendly · Simple request "Sorry, could you say that again?"
Friendly · Checks meaning "Just to make sure I understood — you mean…?"
Professional · Neutral request "Could you clarify what you mean by…?"
Formal · Detailed explanation "I'm afraid I'm not entirely clear on that point — could you please expand on it?"
04 Checking Understanding

Sound organised and confident.

Friendly · Confirms understanding "Let me check if I've got that right."
Professional · Restates decisions "If I understand correctly, you'd like us to…"
Formal · Written confirmation "Please confirm if I've understood your instructions correctly."
Formal · Structured summary "Allow me to restate that to ensure we're aligned."
05 Managing Time

Leadership language.

Friendly · Gentle reminder "We're running a bit short on time."
Professional · Manages time "Can we allocate five more minutes to this?"
Formal · Change topic "We'll need to move on due to time constraints."
Formal · Authoritative "For time reasons, this discussion will be concluded."
06 Delegating & Following Up

Professional authority.

Professional · Assigns responsibility "Can you take ownership of this item?"
Professional · Follow-up request "Could you update me on the status of…?"
Formal · Request for closure "Confirmation of completion is required."
Formal · Progress check "Please confirm the current progress against the plan."
